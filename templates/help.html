PROJECT OVERVIEW

HydroWatch — engineering decision support

AI contribution: a Random Forest classifier learns from the provided labeled CSV and returns a prediction for six manually entered readings. Evaluation metrics come from a reproducible stratified holdout split; they do not prove field accuracy.

Civil contribution: network connectivity checks and preliminary pipe-by-pipe hydraulic estimates based on Darcy–Weisbach. The calculations use SI units internally, water properties approximated at 20 °C, and explicit supplied flow values.

System architecture

Browser forms submit validated values to Flask routes. The model service trains and evaluates against the configured CSV; analysis results and user-owned civil networks/assessments are stored in SQLite. Leak alerts are persisted before optional SMTP delivery. The browser only visualizes saved results returned by the authenticated application.

Equations

  • Area: A = πD² / 4
  • Mean velocity: v = Q / A
  • Reynolds number: Re = ρvD / μ
  • Friction loss: hᶠ = f(L/D)(v² / 2g)
  • Minor loss: hᵐ = K(v² / 2g)
  • Pressure/head relation: Δp = ρgΔh (requires suitable boundary and elevation data)

Assumptions and limitations

  • Supplied pipe flows are not hydraulically solved. Flow-balance warnings are only a consistency check.
  • Friction factor uses laminar 64/Re, Swamee–Jain turbulent estimate, and linear interpolation through transition.
  • Hydraulic review flags are screening priorities only: velocity over 2.5 m/s or segment head loss over 1 m. They do not identify a leak.
  • No confirmed leak location is inferred from RPM or a junction label.
  • Manual readings, demo scenarios, and dataset-derived training examples are distinct sources.
  • Hardware integration is future work; no physical sensor or pump is connected.

Demonstration sequence

  1. Sign in and load a normal demonstration scenario, then run an analysis.
  2. Load the suspected-leak demo and inspect the saved result and alert status.
  3. If SMTP is configured, verify delivery to the account email; otherwise see the recorded configuration status.
  4. Open Civil Pipeline Assessment, load its illustrative network, calculate, and export the saved report as CSV.
